# 10 — Thumbnail plan

## Recommended concept

### Visual

- Center: red Taiwan silhouette resting on a treaty page.
- Left: small Japanese flag card with an arrow moving away from Taiwan.
- Right: an empty destination box with a large question mark.
- Background: dark archive desk, no characters.
- One red underline beneath the empty recipient field.

### Korean thumbnail text

```text
누구에게 넘겼나?
```

Alternative with stronger treaty angle:

```text
받는 나라가 없었다
```

Use the first option as the safer and more natural default.

## Layout

- Taiwan occupies 35–42% of thumbnail height.
- Text occupies the upper-right quadrant, maximum two lines.
- Keep at least 90 px safe margin at 1280×720.
- Avoid placing more than two flags.
- Use one question mark only.

## Color

- Background: charcoal blue.
- Treaty: warm ivory.
- Taiwan: muted red.
- Text: white with a narrow dark shadow.
- Question mark: warm yellow.

## Avoid

- Current political leaders.
- Military explosions or invasion imagery.
- Copying History Matters character design.
- Four or more flags competing for attention.
- Legalistic text too small to read on mobile.
- Claiming that the treaty “gave” Taiwan to a named government.

## Thumbnail A/B pair

### A — Question-led

- Text: `누구에게 넘겼나?`
- Strongest element: blank destination field.
- Expected strength: curiosity without overstating the legal conclusion.

### B — Contradiction-led

- Text: `일본은 포기했다`
- Strongest element: Taiwan separated from Japan by a torn treaty line.
- Expected strength: simpler but less distinctive.
