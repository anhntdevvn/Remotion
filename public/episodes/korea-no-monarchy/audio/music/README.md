# Original episode music — 기묘한 기록실

`korea-original-score-v1.mp3` is an original programmatic instrumental arrangement created locally for this episode, at the channel owner's request. It contains no downloaded recording, musical sample, imported MIDI, soundfont, sampled impulse response, or reference-video music. Piano-like, bowed-string-like, plucked and percussion timbres are synthesized with analytic oscillators/envelopes.

Source generator: `src/episodes/korea-no-monarchy/tools/generate_music.py`. Full note event score: `src/episodes/korea-no-monarchy/data/music-score.json`. Provenance/hash: `src/episodes/korea-no-monarchy/planning/music-provenance.json`. Deterministic seed: 19451963. Source master WAV is reproducible and stored under `out/korea-no-monarchy/audio-master/`, not shipped in public assets. MP3, score and source are not git-ignored.

No paid track or external music license is used for this asset. It has not been registered with Content ID. Keeping the source and provenance is evidence of how the track was made; it does not guarantee that an automated matching system will never produce an erroneous claim. Do not describe it as legally guaranteed “claim-proof”.

The score is not a conventional commercial sampled piano/orchestra recording. Five related arrangements change with the narration chapters, and an intentional musical breath occurs at the empty-throne cut. The Studio mix ducks the score under narration. Review by listening to the composition rather than judging music level from the standalone MP3 alone.
